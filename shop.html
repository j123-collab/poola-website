Not a store

A library of things people can make.

Poola Shop is where campaign identity becomes real-world participation: signs on a table, a badge on a jacket, a 3D printed stand, a poster in a window, a shirt made locally.

No payment flow or product-buying language. Each drop is a free file set designed to help supporters carry a Pool into their neighbourhood, event, studio or workspace.

Delete your Poola account

You can delete your Poola account and its data at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open the Poola app and sign in
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account
  3. Confirm — deletion takes effect immediately

What gets deleted

Your sign-in account is permanently removed and your profile is anonymized: name, email address, phone number, biography and profile photo are erased. You will no longer be able to sign in, and your personal information is no longer associated with any activity.

What is kept

Records that other people depend on — pools you created and contributions made to them — remain, attributed to an anonymized account, so that backers' contribution history and the integrity of completed campaigns is preserved. Financial transaction records are retained as required by bookkeeping and anti-money-laundering law (processed by Stripe).

Can't access the app?

Email info@poola.io from the address on your account and we will process the deletion manually within 30 days.

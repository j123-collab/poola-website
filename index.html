Meet Poola

Giving that goes beyond funding.

Every Poola campaign is a Pool — and people can dive in however they’re able. Pledge money, yes, but also lend a skill, donate an item, volunteer an hour, sign a petition or simply show up.

We measure it all as two numbers on every Pool: Funds and People Power — so impact is finally about more than cash.

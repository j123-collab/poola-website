How it works

Start a Pool. Attract a crowd. Make waves.

Poola turns any cause, project or idea into a Pool — then makes it effortless for people to back it with money and everything else they have to give.

The big idea

Two numbers on every Pool: Funds & People Power.

Money matters — but so do skills, time, resources and voices. Poola tracks both, so a Pool that’s 17% funded and 83% powered by people is finally a success story you can see.

Pool Starters launch the campaign. Poolers dive in to back it. Everyone can watch the progress, the updates and the impact in real time.

Ways to dive in

Six ways to back the Pools you love.

Fund

Contribute money in seconds — one-off or recurring — with a single, transparent fee.

Offer skills

Graphic design, IT, legal advice, translation, carpentry — lend what you’re great at.

Offer resources

Donate items a Pool needs — furniture, cameras, books, kitchen kit and more.

Volunteer

Pick a time slot and show up in person to help a Pool reach its goal.

Sign

Add your name to petitions and lend your voice to the causes that move you.

Take part

Join events, meet-ups and live moments that bring a Pool’s community together.

Discover

NewsPool

About Poola

We believe support should compound.

Many small actions can become a movement when they are visible, trusted and easy to join. Poola is built for that moment.

Mission

Make it easier for people to back the things they want to exist.

Poola started from a simple frustration: most crowd-sourcing tools only measure money, even when the real momentum is much broader.

We are building a platform where funds, skills, resources, time and voice can all be part of the same campaign, with transparent progress and an experience people actually want to return to.

Clean, useful, transparent.

Our mission

To unify the world of crowd-sourcing — and make it accessible from anywhere, by anyone.

Poola began with a simple belief: people want to give more than money to the things they care about. We’re building the platform that finally lets them.

The story

Built once. Coming back stronger.

Poola started in Sweden with a small team, an angel-backed pre-seed round and a big idea. We designed the full experience, built our MVP on Flutter and Stripe, and proved that crowd-sourcing — not just crowd-funding — is the future.

Then we sharpened the vision. Now we’re relaunching: same mission, clearer product, and a waitlist of people ready to make waves with us.
